# Privacy Policy (TravelMoney)

**[Flosis]** (the "Developer") establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy in order to protect the personal information of users and to handle related complaints promptly and smoothly.

---

### 1. Purpose of Processing
The Developer processes personal information for the following purposes:
1. **Travel Record Management**: Travel itineraries, expense/income history, and roadmap logs.
2. **Service Provision**: Exchange rate calculation, location-based address search, and data backup/restore.
3. **Customer Support**: Responding to bug reports and user inquiries.

### 2. Items to be Processed
1. **Location Information**: GPS data for recording roadmap paths and locations (Optional).
2. **Media/Files**: Access to device storage for saving receipt and roadmap photos.
3. **Camera**: Capturing photos within the app.
4. **Device Info**: App version, device model, and OS version (included in inquiry emails).
5. **Billing Info**: Google Play billing ID and purchase history for Premium features.

### 3. Third-Party Provision
The Developer does not provide personal information to third parties, except for processing payments through the **Google Play Billing System**.

### 4. Retention and Storage (Local Storage)
1. **All app data is stored locally on the user's device.**
2. The Developer does not store or collect user data on external servers. All data is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

### 5. Limitation of Liability
Since all data is stored locally, the Developer is not responsible for data loss due to device loss, app deletion, or failure to perform backups.
